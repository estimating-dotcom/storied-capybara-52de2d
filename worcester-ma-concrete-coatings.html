Worcester & Central MA Flooring FAQ

Common Questions in Worcester & Central MA

Answers on turnaround schedules, surface preparation, moisture mitigation, and commercial estimating across Worcester, Framingham & Central Massachusetts.

What types of facilities does LionStone coat in Worcester, Westborough, and Framingham?
We coat CNC machine shops, plastics and metal fabrication plants, biotech laboratories, food commissaries, multi-story commercial stairwells, auto service bays, and residential garages throughout Worcester County and MetroWest.
Can you repair badly pitted or delaminating factory concrete before coating?
Yes. We mechanically strip failed paint or mastics via planetary diamond grinding or shot blasting, route active cracks, rebuild spalled joints and expansion trenches with structural polyurea mortar, and apply high-build epoxy.
What is the best floor system for Central MA cold storage or food processing facilities?
Three-component cementitious urethane mortar installed at 3/16 to 3/8 inch provides matching thermal expansion to concrete, withstanding freeze-thaw cycling and 212°F+ washdowns.
