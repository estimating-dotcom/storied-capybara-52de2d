Our Process

How We Engineer & Execute Every Floor

From initial substrate diagnostics to rapid return-to-service curing, every LionStone installation follows a strict 5-stage industrial protocol.

First

Assess & Profile Substrate.

We evaluate moisture vapor emission (MVE) and mechanically profile the slab using 3-phase HEPA planetary diamond grinders (CSP 2–5).

Second

Rebuild Spalls & Joints.

Structural polymer mortars and elastomeric polyurea fillers rebuild spalled concrete, trenches, and expansion joints flush with the slab.

Third

Engineer the Base Bond.

100% solids penetrating epoxy, novolac chemical primers, or moisture-mitigation barriers fuse permanently into the open concrete pores.

Fourth

Broadcast & Build.

High-build self-leveling slurries, sanitary cove bases, or full-refusal industrial quartz and vinyl flake aggregates are applied to spec.

Fifth

Back in Business.

High-wear polyurethane or UV-stable polyaspartic topcoats lock the system—getting your facility back online with minimal downtime.

Engineering & Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Commercial & Facility Questions

Answers on resin chemistry, forklift cure times, ICRI diamond profiling, moisture mitigation, and service across Massachusetts & Connecticut.

What is the difference between 100% solids epoxy and cementitious urethane mortar?
100% solids industrial epoxy is ideal for warehouses, manufacturing bays, and distribution hubs needing high compressive strength (10,500+ PSI) and abrasion resistance. Three-component cementitious urethane mortar (3/16" to 3/8") has a thermal expansion coefficient matched to concrete, withstanding 212°F+ live steam washdowns, freeze-thaw thermal shock, and organic food acids.
How long does industrial epoxy take to cure before forklift traffic?
Light foot traffic is typically permitted in 12 to 18 hours, with full mechanical forklift and steel-wheel loading returning in 24 to 48 hours depending on resin thickness and ambient slab temperatures. When minimal plant downtime is critical, fast-cure polyaspartic and urethane formulations accelerate return to service.
Why does LionStone use ICRI CSP diamond grinding instead of acid etching?
Acid etching leaves behind chemical salts that weaken adhesion and fails to remove surface laitance or old mastics. LionStone uses 3-phase planetary diamond grinders and shot blasters paired with HEPA dust extractors to mechanically profile concrete to ICRI CSP 2–5 standards, creating open concrete pores for permanent mechanical bonding.
Can you install coatings over oily, spalled, or high-moisture concrete slabs?
Yes. We perform ASTM calcium chloride and relative humidity moisture testing prior to coating, apply oil-stop or 25-lb hydrostatic moisture vapor barrier primers where needed, and rebuild spalled control joints, floor trenches, and damaged treads with structural polyurea and polymer mortar.
