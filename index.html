Our Process

How We Engineer & Execute Every Floor

From initial substrate diagnostics to rapid return-to-service curing, every LionStone installation follows a strict 5-stage industrial protocol.

First

Assess & Profile Substrate.

We evaluate moisture vapor emission (MVE) and mechanically profile the slab using 3-phase HEPA planetary diamond grinders (CSP 2–5).

Second

Rebuild Spalls & Joints.

Structural polymer mortars and elastomeric polyurea fillers rebuild spalled concrete, trenches, and expansion joints flush with the slab.

Third

Engineer the Base Bond.

100% solids penetrating epoxy, novolac chemical primers, or moisture-mitigation barriers fuse permanently into the open concrete pores.

Fourth

Broadcast & Build.

High-build self-leveling slurries, sanitary cove bases, or full-refusal industrial quartz and vinyl flake aggregates are applied to spec.

Fifth

Back in Business.

High-wear polyurethane or UV-stable polyaspartic topcoats lock the system—getting your facility back online with minimal downtime.
