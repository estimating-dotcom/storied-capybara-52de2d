The LionStone Standard™

Our 5-Step Craftsmanship Process

We never take shortcuts. Every project is executed with industrial equipment and rigorous quality control for a bond that lasts decades.

1

Diamond Grinding

Heavy planetary diamond grinding with HEPA dust containment opens the concrete pores (CSP-2 standard).

2

Crack & Spall Mending

Pure polyurea and epoxy fillers repair cracks, pitting, and expansion joints flush with the slab surface.

3

Deep-Penetrating Base

Industrial 100% solids epoxy or moisture-mitigating primer fuses deep inside the prepared concrete substrate.

4

Full Flake Broadcast

Full-refusal vinyl or quartz broadcast provides rich depth, texture, and non-slip traction throughout.

5

Polyaspartic Topcoat

Crystal clear, non-yellowing polyaspartic topcoat seals the floor with extreme chemical and hot tire resistance.
