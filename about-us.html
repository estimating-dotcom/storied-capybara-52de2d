Why Commercial Facilities Choose LionStone

In industrial and commercial environments, floor failures cause dangerous OSHA hazards and costly production stoppages. We established LionStone Floors to give facility managers, plant supervisors, and property operators a trusted contractor focused on technical precision, proper surface profiling, and zero-compromise chemical formulations.

Our approach begins where inferior contractors cut corners: mechanical surface preparation. We deploy heavy 3-phase planetary diamond grinders and HEPA dust extractors to open the concrete profile to ICRI CSP-2 to CSP-4 standards, ensuring our 100% solid polyaspartic and epoxy basecoats fuse permanently with the concrete slab.
