Why We Started LionStone Floors

Too many property owners in Massachusetts and Connecticut have experienced peeling DIY epoxy paints, bubbling garage coatings, and unreturned contractor calls. We built LionStone Floors to set a new standard of reliability, technical precision, and longevity.

Our approach begins where others cut corners: mechanical surface preparation. We invest in heavy industrial planetary diamond grinders and HEPA dust extractors to open the concrete profile to exact CSP-2 specifications, ensuring our 100% solid polyaspartic and epoxy basecoats fuse permanently with the concrete slab.
