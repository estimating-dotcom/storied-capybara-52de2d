Moisture Mitigation

Basement Epoxy & Moisture Vapor Barrier

Subterranean basements often suffer from hydrostatic pressure and damp concrete slabs. Our deep-penetrating epoxy moisture mitigation primer blocks ground moisture from pushing through the slab, preventing musty odors, mold, and coating delamination.

  • Blocks up to 25 lbs of Hydrostatic MVE: Eliminates dampness and efflorescence.
  • Mold & Mildew Resistant: Creates a sanitary, easily cleaned living space.
  • Available in Solid Colors, Flake Blends, or Warm Earth Tones.
