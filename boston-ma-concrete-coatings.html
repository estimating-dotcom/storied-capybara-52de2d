Greater Boston & Eastern MA Flooring FAQ

Common Questions in Greater Boston & Eastern MA

Answers on turnaround schedules, surface preparation, moisture mitigation, and commercial estimating across Greater Boston, Route 128 & Eastern Massachusetts.

Are your epoxy and urethane coatings zero-VOC for occupied Boston and Cambridge buildings?
Yes. Our 100% solids cycloaliphatic epoxy, Novolac, and polyaspartic systems are zero- or ultra-low-VOC and odorless during cure—ideal for hospitals, occupied office towers, universities, and life-sciences buildings.
Do you restore concrete commercial stairwells and exterior access ramps in Greater Boston?
Yes. Multi-story stair towers and ADA ramps are one of our core specialties. We rebuild chipped concrete risers and treads with polymer mortar and broadcast non-slip quartz with safety yellow nosing.
Do you mobilize HEPA dust-extraction grinders into tight Greater Boston sites?
Yes. Our planetary grinders pair directly with industrial HEPA vacuum systems so occupied commercial corridors, labs, and retail properties stay completely dust-free.
Can LionStone partner with Boston General Contractors on commercial build-outs?
Yes. We provide rapid Division 09 sub-pricing, architectural specification matching, and flexible night or weekend shifts to help Eastern MA GCs hit tight TCO deadlines.
