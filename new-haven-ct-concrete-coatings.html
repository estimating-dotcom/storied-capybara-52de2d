Common Questions in New Haven & Southern CT

Answers on turnaround schedules, surface preparation, moisture mitigation, and commercial estimating across New Haven, Wallingford & Southern Connecticut.

Does coastal humidity in Southern Connecticut affect concrete floor coatings?
Yes, if improperly prepped. Subterranean and coastal CT slabs frequently exhibit high moisture vapor emission (MVE). We perform ASTM moisture testing and apply deep-penetrating epoxy vapor barrier primers capable of resisting up to 25 lbs of hydrostatic pressure.
Which Southern Connecticut industrial and commercial areas do you cover?
We cover the entire I-91 South, Route 8, and I-95 corridors including Middletown, Meriden, Wallingford, North Haven, New Haven, Hamden, Milford, Shelton, Bridgeport, Stratford, Norwalk, and Stamford.
Can you coat commercial kitchens and food-grade facilities in New Haven County?
Yes. Our USDA/FDA-ready cementitious urethane mortar systems resist organic acids, vegetable oils, and high-temperature live steam washdowns without delaminating.
